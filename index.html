I am a Master's student in Computer Science at Arizona State University. I am currently a research intern in the Data Mining and Reinforcement Learning (DaRL) Group, advised by Prof. Hua Wei.

My research focuses on LLM agents and multi-agent systems. I am especially interested in making agents reliable and controllable: understanding what makes agent behavior predictable, and building evaluations that measure it rigorously.

Before ASU, I received my B.Eng. in Information Security from Changchun University of Science and Technology, and spent a year as a software engineer building computer-vision and data systems for electronics manufacturing.
